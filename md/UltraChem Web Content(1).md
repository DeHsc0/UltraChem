ABOUT ULTRACHEM 
Building Infrastructure with Consistency 

UltraChem Infra Solutions Pvt. Ltd., headquartered in Nagpur, Maharashtra, is a 
professionally managed construction chemicals company delivering high-performance, 
technically advanced, and cost-efficient solutions for modern infrastructure and 
building projects across Nation.  

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Backed by over 10 years of industry leadership and technical management experience, 
UltraChem specializes in enhancing the durability, structural integrity, waterprooﬁng 
performance, and service life of concrete structures.  

We operate with a solution-driven approach — combining quality-controlled 
manufacturing, technical expertise, and on-site support — enabling us to serve 
government departments, infrastructure contractors, industrial developers, and leading 
construction companies. UltraChem is committed to contributing to India’s growing 
infrastructure through reliable, performance-oriented construction chemical system . 

VISION & MISSION 

Vision 
To become a leading and trusted construction chemicals brand recognized for quality 
excellence, consistency, technical innovation, and long-term performance across 
infrastructure and building sectors. 

Mission 
To deliver superior-quality construction chemical products built on research and 
performance standards 

CORE COMPETENCE 

Engineered for Real Site Challenges 

UltraChem focuses on performance-enhancing chemical solutions for:  
• Concrete durability enhancement  
• Waterprooﬁng and moisture protection  
• Corrosion protection of reinforcement steel  
• Structural life extension systems  
• Repair and rehabilitation solutions.  

Our products are engineered to address real site challenges such as permeability, 
shrinkage cracking, corrosion exposure, harsh weather conditions, and aggressive 
environmental effects.
