UltraChem Company Profile
ABOUT ULTRACHEM
Building Infrastructure with Consistency

UltraChem Infra Solutions Pvt. Ltd., headquartered in Nagpur, Maharashtra, is a professionally managed construction chemicals company delivering high-performance, technically advanced, and cost-efficient solutions for modern infrastructure and building projects across the Nation.

Backed by over 10 years of industry leadership and technical management experience, UltraChem specializes in enhancing the durability, structural integrity, waterproofing performance, and service life of concrete structures.

We operate with a solution-driven approach — combining quality-controlled manufacturing, technical expertise, and on-site support — enabling us to serve government departments, infrastructure contractors, industrial developers, and leading construction companies. UltraChem is committed to contributing to India’s growing infrastructure through reliable, performance-oriented construction chemical systems.

Compatibility Engineering

Our products are engineered to address real site challenges such as permeability, shrinkage cracking, corrosion exposure, harsh weather conditions, and aggressive environmental effects.

Product Category,Description
Concrete Admixtures,"High-performance admixtures designed to improve workability, optimize setting time, enhance compressive strength, and ensure consistent concrete quality."
Crystalline Waterproofing Systems,"Integral waterproofing admixtures that react within concrete to form insoluble crystals, permanently blocking capillaries and micro-cracks."
Corrosion Inhibitors,"Advanced solutions that protect reinforcement steel from chloride-induced and environmental corrosion, significantly increasing structural service life."
Curing Compounds,"Efficient curing systems that reduce water evaporation, minimize shrinkage cracks, and promote proper hydration for optimal strength."
Joint Sealants,"Durable, flexible, and weather-resistant sealants suitable for expansion joints, industrial flooring, façades, and infrastructure structures."
Comprehensive Waterproofing,"Systems designed for terraces, basements, foundations, podium slabs, water tanks, wet areas, and other moisture-prone zones."
